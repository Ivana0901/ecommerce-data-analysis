# KPI Definitions

This document defines the primary metrics used throughout the SQL analysis,
Python analysis, and Power BI dashboard.

## Reporting Scope

- Reporting period: January 1, 2017 through August 31, 2018
- Date field: `order_purchase_timestamp`
- Successfully completed sales are represented by orders with
  `order_status = 'delivered'`
- Revenue represents product item value and excludes freight
- Total order value includes product item value and freight
- Order-level calculations use one record per `order_id`
- Customer-level calculations use `customer_unique_id`
- Review calculations exclude orders without review scores
- Delivery calculations exclude missing or negative delivery durations

## Sales KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Delivered Orders | Number of successfully delivered orders during the reporting period | Count of distinct `order_id` where `order_status = 'delivered'` |
| Delivered Revenue | Product item value generated by delivered orders | Sum of `price` for delivered orders |
| Delivered Freight | Freight value associated with delivered orders | Sum of `freight_value` for delivered orders |
| Delivered Total Value | Combined product and freight value for delivered orders | Delivered revenue + delivered freight |
| Average Order Value | Average product revenue generated per delivered order | Delivered revenue / delivered orders |
| Order Status Share | Percentage of orders associated with each order status | Orders in status / total orders × 100 |
| Monthly Revenue Growth | Percentage change in delivered revenue compared with the previous month | `(current month revenue - previous month revenue) / previous month revenue × 100` |

## Customer KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Unique Customers | Number of distinct customers with at least one delivered order | Count of distinct `customer_unique_id` |
| Average Orders per Customer | Average delivered-order frequency per customer | Delivered orders / unique customers |
| One-Time Customer | Customer with exactly one delivered order during the reporting period | Customer delivered-order count = 1 |
| Repeat Customer | Customer with two or more delivered orders during the reporting period | Customer delivered-order count >= 2 |
| Repeat Customer Rate | Percentage of customers classified as repeat customers | Repeat customers / unique customers × 100 |
| Customer State Order Share | Percentage of delivered orders originating from each customer state | State delivered orders / total delivered orders × 100 |

## Customer Experience KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Average Review Score | Average review score among orders with available reviews | Average of `avg_review_score` |
| Delivery Time | Number of calendar days between purchase and customer delivery | Delivered customer date - purchase date |
| Average Delivery Days | Average delivery duration among valid delivered orders | Average of non-negative delivery days |
| Delivery Bucket Review Score | Average review score for orders grouped by delivery duration | Average review score within each delivery-time bucket |

## Delivery-Time Buckets

- 0–3 days
- 4–7 days
- 8–14 days
- 15–21 days
- 22 or more days

## Important Interpretation Notes

Order item value associated with canceled, unavailable, processing, or other
non-delivered statuses is not treated as finalized revenue.

The main revenue KPIs use delivered orders only.

Revenue excludes freight unless the metric explicitly states that freight is
included.

The incomplete months outside January 2017 through August 2018 are excluded
from the primary dashboard analysis to avoid distorted monthly comparisons.
